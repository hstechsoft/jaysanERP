BRIEF ABOUT JAYSAN ROTAVATOR TRACTOR IN INDIA

The JAYSAN ROTAVATOR is one of the most popular tractors in India, coming in the 35-40 HP category. This tractor comes in the lower middle price range, i.e. Rs 5-7 Lakhs. The JAYSAN ROTAVATOR has a 4-stroke, direct injection, water-cooled engine that facilitates the functioning of the tractor even in remote areas without interruptions. It has all the features and specifications of an ideal tractor in the price segment. Let’s discuss more about the JAYSAN ROTAVATOR.
