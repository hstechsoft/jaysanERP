What is a Rotavator?

A Rotavator or a Tiller rotary tiller is a tractor-drawn tool designed for seedbed arrangement within one or two ways for sowing seeds. It reduced the design of traditional tillage operations. A Rotavator machine is a piece of powerful machinery used in farms to break and mix the soil planting seeds.

A rotary tiller tractor has one of the most effective engine power. It directly works to the soil with no wheel slip and offers a fundamental reduction in transmission power loss. Rotavator plough is most suitable for separating and mixing residuals of maize, wheat, sugarcane, etc. It helps in improving the health of the soil and saves fuel, cost, and time.
